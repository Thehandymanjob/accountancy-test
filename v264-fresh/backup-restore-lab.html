Do not run this legacy test. It tested one combined Accounting + Projects backup and a global reset. That workflow is prohibited by the current architecture and has been retired.

The active v264 application now uses separate, domain-scoped backup and restore files. Accounting (including invoice numbering) and Projects (including Company Contracts & Jobs) must always be backed up and restored independently.
